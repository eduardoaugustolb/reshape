# ReShape Agent Instructions & Operational Directives

## Identity and Mission
ReShape is an agent-native 3D modeling and sculpting protocol designed for Blender. It provides structured guidance and tool primitives for autonomous agents that create, refine, and audit 3D meshes.

## When to Use This Tool
Reach for ReShape whenever:
1. An incoming brief asks to create a 3D model (character, prop, environmental asset, organic or hard-surface) inside Blender.
2. The user supplies an image or sketch and wants a corresponding 3D polygonal asset.
3. You need to inspect or repair an existing mesh (checking non-manifold edges, self-intersections, inverted normals, or poor polygon flow).
4. You need multi-pass comparison renders where camera angles (front, profile, three-quarter), lighting setups, and focal lengths must remain identical across revisions.
5. The model requires specialized anatomy standards (facial structure, hands, joints) or specific aesthetic styles (faceted low-poly, cel-shaded anime, claymation, stylized cartoon).

## Negative Constraints (When NOT to Use)
- Do not use for 2D graphics generation, illustration, or photo editing without 3D geometry.
- Do not use for pure text summarization, language translation, or general coding unrelated to 3D and Blender.
- Do not use for mechanical engineering FEA stress simulations or non-polygonal NURBS CAD.

## Step-by-Step Agent Workflow
1. **Analyze Reference & Establish Observable Criteria**: Extract silhouette markers, volume ratios, and landmarks. Note occluded areas as "inferred".
2. **Pass 1 - Silhouette & Blockout**: Construct primary primitive volumes. Confirm contour against reference before proceeding.
3. **Pass 2 - Secondary Masses & Transitions**: Refine junctions (shoulders, neck, organic blend zones). Ensure clean edge loops.
4. **Pass 3 - Surface & Detail**: Sculpt grooves, surface textures, and fine features.
5. **Multi-View Comparison**: Render matching orthographic and perspective views. Isolate discrepancies.
6. **Topology & Mesh Verification**: Run the local ReShape audit script on the saved .blend and inspect the matched views. Check closure and thickness for print or deformation for rigging where required. Hosted mesh validation returns `unverified`.
