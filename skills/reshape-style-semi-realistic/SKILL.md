# Semi-realistic modeling

Use [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md) for evidence-based approval and [the styles overview](../reshape-styles/SKILL.md) for mixed briefs.

## Visual references

- [Blender Studio Rain head sculpt](https://studio.blender.org/training/stylized-character-workflow/5d358d1acb14814e3f8f3fde/). Compare the simplified facial planes with the retained eye socket and jaw structure.
- [Blender Studio Rain and Snow final renders](https://studio.blender.org/training/stylized-character-workflow/chapter/5d2890c4f6c3a9c450d57b1f/). Study how adjacent shapes retain a consistent level of simplification across each character.

Use these examples to study visual decisions. Use the user's own target for likeness, dimensions, and design requirements.

## Modeling pass

Mark which anatomical or structural relationships stay close to the reference. Alter named masses or plane transitions deliberately. Keep the simplification level consistent in adjacent regions. Carry the same large-to-small hierarchy into hair, hands, clothing, or prop seams.

## Approval checks

Compare a neutral render beside the target and mark preserved landmarks and deliberate departures. Compare the face and hands at the same framing to detect uneven simplification.

Reject and correct these failures before approval. Naturalistic joints adjacent to arbitrarily inflated anatomy, missing depth in profile, or a blanket smoothing pass that erases identity. Record any remaining issue as partial delivery with a named view and cause.
