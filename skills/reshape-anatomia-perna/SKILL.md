# Lower leg and calf

Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 8.18, tibia and fibula](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-4-bones-of-the-lower-limb). Trace the anterior tibial crest and distal malleoli.
- [OpenStax Figures 11.32 and 11.33, lower-leg muscle plates](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-6-appendicular-muscles-of-the-pelvic-girdle-and-lower-limbs). Compare the posterior calf against the anterior and lateral compartments.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Give the anterior tibial crest a distinct plane. Taper the posterior calf mass into the Achilles tendon. Match calf asymmetry to the target's rear view.

## Evidence to capture

Capture side and back from knee through heel. Inspect calf taper and Achilles continuity at the same ankle angle as the reference.

## Review and rejection

Compare side and rear views. Examine Achilles continuity to the calcaneus during plantar flexion where applicable.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. An untapered round calf, a tibial crest on the wrong side, or a tendon detached from the heel.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
