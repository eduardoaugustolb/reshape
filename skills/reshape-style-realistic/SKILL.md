# Realistic modeling

Use [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md) for evidence-based approval and [the styles overview](../reshape-estilos/SKILL.md) for mixed briefs.

## Visual references

- [Digital Reality Lab sample head scans](https://www.digitalrealitylab.com/sample-model/). Study the neutral scan's cheek-to-jaw and eyelid transitions from multiple angles; derive proportions from the actual subject.
- [Khronos Damaged Helmet render and model](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/DamagedHelmet). Study the relationship between structural silhouette and localized surface damage.

Use these examples to study visual decisions. Use the user's own target for likeness, dimensions, and design requirements.

## Modeling pass

Establish scale and landmarks against aligned source views. Block primary masses and solve structural junctions. For portraits, model eyelid thickness, lip contact, and skin transitions at clay stage. For manufactured objects, check wall thickness, joins, and contact before texture.

## Approval checks

Compare clay front, profile, and three-quarter views to the target before adding pores or wear. Confirm hard-surface highlights travel continuously over intended bevels. Repeat under broad diffuse and grazing light.

Reject and correct these failures before approval. A likeness that disappears in clay, abrupt thickness changes, floating parts, or material effects that hide a form error. Record any remaining issue as partial delivery with a named view and cause.
