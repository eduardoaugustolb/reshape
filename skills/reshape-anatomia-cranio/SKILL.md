# Skull and cranial vault

Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 7.5 and 7.9, lateral and posterior skull](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-2-the-skull). Compare the temporal transition with the posterior vault.
- [Digital Reality Lab head scans](https://www.digitalrealitylab.com/sample-model/). Assess how the vault and temple read under soft tissue.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Distinguish frontal, parietal, and occipital masses and place the temporal hollow above the zygomatic arch. Build the orbits in relation to the braincase.

## Evidence to capture

Inspect a clean top silhouette with hair hidden where practical. Use the lateral and rear views to diagnose a flattened occiput versus merely low or asymmetric hair volume.

## Review and rejection

Compare top, side, and back views. Locate the target's widest point. For an exposed skull, verify the continuity of the arches and orbital rims.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. A spherical vault with no lateral transition or zygomatic arches that intrude into the orbit.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
