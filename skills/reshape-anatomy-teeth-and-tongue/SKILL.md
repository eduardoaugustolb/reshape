# Teeth, gums, and tongue

Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 23.7, 23.8, and 23.10, oral cavity and dentition](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus). Compare a top view of the tongue with the teeth arranged along the arches.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Place separate dental arches and seat the teeth in the gums. Distinguish incisors, canines, and posterior teeth as indicated by the target. Seat the tongue within the floor of the mouth.

## Evidence to capture

Isolate the upper and lower arches for a plan view, then repeat in the full asset. Add an open-mouth crosscheck for gums and tongue if those surfaces are visible.

## Review and rejection

Inspect both dental arches from inside the mouth and check contact in the closed pose. If rigged, inspect tongue and gum intersections when the mouth opens.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Floating teeth, crossed arches, or a tongue intersecting the teeth.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
