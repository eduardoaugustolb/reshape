# Lips and vermilion

For integration with the surrounding anatomy, see [the regional overview](mouth.md).

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 23.7, mouth](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus). Use the anterior view for lip relationships to cheeks and oral cavity.
- [FaceBase lip landmarks](https://www.facebase.org/resources/human/facial_norms/notes/). Locate midpoint and mouth corners for consistent alignment.

## Modeling

Define the skin-to-vermilion transition, lip roll, meeting line, and corner depth. Build the upper and lower masses around the target's oral curvature and resolve contact in the neutral pose.

## Evidence and rejection

Compare side and three-quarter crops, then open and seal the mouth if rigged. Inspect how the lips turn inward at the corners.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. Flat painted lips, lips that pass through one another, or a crease that continues through the cheek without support. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
