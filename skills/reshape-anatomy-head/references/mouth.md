# Mouth and lips

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 23.7, mouth](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus). Locate the lips and the boundaries between the oral cavity and surrounding tissue.
- [FaceBase upper and lower lip landmarks](https://www.facebase.org/resources/human/facial_norms/notes/). Use the surface points to align the opening and mouth corners.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Support the lips on the curve of the underlying dental and alveolar structures. Model lip thickness, the philtrum, the contact line, and corners in continuity with cheeks and chin.

## Evidence to capture

Capture the relaxed contact line and the widest required opening. Verify the two corners close cleanly and the lips retain thickness in profile.

## Review and rejection

Check front, profile, and three-quarter views. If rigged, test closure, smiling, and opening, inspecting the mouth corners and tooth contact.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Flat lips, pierced mouth corners, or a painted opening on a model that must open its mouth.
