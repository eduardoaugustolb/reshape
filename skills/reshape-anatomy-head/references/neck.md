# Neck

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 7.25, cervical vertebrae](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-3-the-vertebral-column). Locate the cervical axis and posterior elements.
- [OpenStax neck muscle figures](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-3-axial-muscles-of-the-head-neck-and-back). Compare sternocleidomastoid orientation and the transitions at the skull and clavicle.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Relate the occipital base to the cervical spine. Place sternocleidomastoid between the mastoid and its sternal and clavicular attachments, and trapezius posteriorly. Match their surface visibility to the subject.

## Evidence to capture

Use front and profile to trace mastoid-to-clavicle direction. If rigged, inspect the nape and side contour during rotation and tilt.

## Review and rejection

Inspect side and back views and, if rigged, rotation and tilt. Check the transitions into the nape and clavicles.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. A cylinder attached to the chin, rigid symmetrical tendons during rotation, or a gap at the nape.
