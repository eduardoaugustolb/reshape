# Forehead and eyebrows

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 7.4 and 7.5, skull](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-2-the-skull). Locate the glabella and superior orbital margin.
- [OpenStax Figures 11.7 and 11.8, expression muscles](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-3-axial-muscles-of-the-head-neck-and-back). Compare regions affected by brow elevation and contraction.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Distinguish forehead convexity, glabella, and supraorbital rim. Place the brows on the surface seen in the reference and preserve the transition into the temple.

## Evidence to capture

Use three-quarter crops with diffuse and side light. Mark glabella, supraorbital turn, and temporal plane so a brow shadow cannot stand in for shape.

## Review and rejection

Check side and three-quarter views under soft side lighting. If rigged, compare skin at rest, with raised brows, and with furrowed brows.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Floating brows or a rigid brow arch that erases the transition to the orbit.
