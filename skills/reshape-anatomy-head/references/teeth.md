For integration with the surrounding anatomy, see [the regional overview](teeth-and-tongue.md).

- [OpenStax Figures 23.10 and 23.11, dentition and tooth structure](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus). Compare arch layout and crown shapes before adding detail.

## Modeling

Set the maxillary and mandibular arches and fit the crowns to the target's dentition and age. Integrate gingival transitions and assign the lower arch to the jaw if rigged.

## Evidence and rejection

Capture both arches occlusally and their contact from the side. Recheck the target smile and open-mouth angle with lips visible.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. Floating crowns, uniform tooth blocks, crossed arch placement, or lower teeth that fail to follow the jaw. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
