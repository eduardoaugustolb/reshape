# Eyes and eyelids

## Visual references

- [Orbital Anatomy for the Surgeon, orbital and eyelid figures](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3566239/). Inspect the orbital rim and where the upper and lower lid margins attach.
- [FaceBase ocular landmarks](https://www.facebase.org/resources/human/facial_norms/notes/). Identify inner and outer corners before aligning the views.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Seat the globe in the orbit and model the slight corneal projection if the scale warrants it. Wrap the lids around the globe with readable margins that join at the medial and lateral canthi.

## Evidence to capture

Capture straight gaze and three-quarter views at the same eye height. If rigged, add a closed lid crop and an off-axis gaze crop showing margin contact.

## Review and rejection

Check in front and three-quarter views that the eyes aim at the intended target. If rigged, test opening, closing, and gaze shifts for gaps or penetration.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Globes pasted onto the face, lids clipping the cornea, or gaps when the eyes should be closed.
