# Tongue and oral floor

For integration with the surrounding anatomy, see [the regional overview](teeth-and-tongue.md).

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 23.7 and 23.8, oral cavity and tongue](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus). Compare the superior tongue surface with its placement inside the mouth.
- [OpenStax Figure 11.11, tongue anatomy](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-3-axial-muscles-of-the-head-neck-and-back). Locate attachment and visible volume before planning poses.

## Modeling

Build a tapered muscular volume anchored to the oral floor. Distinguish midline, sides, and tip. Add papillae only where resolution and references justify them.

## Evidence and rejection

Inspect the cavity from above and in an open-mouth three-quarter view. If required, test tongue elevation and protrusion for tooth and palate contact.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. An unattached tongue, tubular shape without taper, or unintended protrusion through teeth, floor, or lips. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
