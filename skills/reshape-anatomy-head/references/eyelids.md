# Eyelids and canthi

For integration with the surrounding anatomy, see [the regional overview](eyes.md).

## Visual references

- [Orbital Anatomy for the Surgeon, eyelid and canthal figures](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3566239/). Find the medial and lateral attachments and inspect the lid margin.
- [FaceBase ocular landmark examples](https://www.facebase.org/resources/human/facial_norms/notes/). Identify inner and outer canthi before comparing eyelid opening.

## Modeling

Lay both margins against the globe and connect them at the canthi. Build the exposed margin thickness and choose the upper fold and lower lid-to-cheek transition from the subject.

## Evidence and rejection

Capture straight gaze and three-quarter views, plus full closure if rigged. Check the corner under a plain material with lashes hidden during diagnosis.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. Paper-thin margins in a close-up, lid clipping, broken corners, or a slit in the fully closed pose. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
