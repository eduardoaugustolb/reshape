# Whole head

Obtain the [required visual reference](../reshape/SKILL.md#required-visual-reference) before modeling or visual review. Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 7.4, 7.5, and 7.9, multiview skull](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-2-the-skull). Follow the relative positions of orbit, jaw, and occiput.
- [Digital Reality Lab head scan sample gallery](https://www.digitalrealitylab.com/sample-model/). Compare the back of the head with the face and locate the ear and neck attachment on a surface model.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Build the cranial vault and its posterior volume. Connect the zygomatic bones, maxilla, and mandible, seat the ears laterally, and join the neck beneath the cranial base.

## Evidence to capture

Capture an overhead silhouette plus profile. Compare posterior mass and the depth of the facial wedge. Recapture the ear and neck joints before signing off.

## Review and rejection

Check the silhouette from crown to occiput, the mandibular angle, and the depth between face and ear from the side and back.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. A flattened back of the skull, a mask-like face attachment, or a neck joined only under the chin.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
