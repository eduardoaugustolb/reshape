# Globe and corneal profile

Use [the regional overview](../reshape-anatomia-olhos/SKILL.md) for regional integration and [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md) for approval of the saved asset. Confirm surface variation and proportions against the subject reference.

## Visual references

- [Orbital Anatomy for the Surgeon, orbital illustrations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3566239/). Locate globe depth against the orbital rim.
- [Clinical anatomy: cornea and ocular surface](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12528774/). Compare the cornea, limbus, and surrounding ocular surface.

## Modeling

Set the globe orientation from the desired gaze and seat it relative to the orbit. Model the corneal bulge and transition into the sclera at the scale the brief needs. Set the iris and pupil so the gaze reads without relying solely on reflections.

## Evidence and rejection

Use a neutral front view and a profile crop. Rotate gaze if rigged and inspect globe-lid contact.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. Off-target gaze, unsupported globe projection, reversed corneal contour, or intersection in the required gaze range. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
