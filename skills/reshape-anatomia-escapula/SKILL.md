# Scapula and shoulder blade

Use [the regional overview](../reshape-anatomia-costas/SKILL.md) for regional integration and [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md) for approval of the saved asset. Confirm surface variation and proportions against the subject reference.

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 8.3 and 8.4, pectoral girdle and scapula](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-1-the-pectoral-girdle). Locate the medial border, scapular spine, and acromion from behind.
- [OpenStax Figure 11.22, muscles that position the pectoral girdle](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-5-muscles-of-the-pectoral-girdle-and-upper-limbs). Compare the structures linking the scapula to the thorax.

## Modeling

Fit the blade over the curved rib cage and blend its landmarks into the surface as the target warrants. Coordinate scapular orientation with arm position.

## Evidence and rejection

Capture back and rear three-quarter views. If rigged, compare arm down, forward reach, and elevation for blade position and skin transition.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. A detached blade, arbitrary highlighted borders, or a fixed scapular pose during the required arm movement. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
