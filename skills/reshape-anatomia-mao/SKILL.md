# Whole hand

Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Modeling

Build the palm arch and thickness around the metacarpals. Integrate the thenar and hypothenar eminences. Place the thumb from its base and check the gap between thumb and index finger.

## Review and rejection

Inspect dorsal, palmar, and lateral views. If rigged, test open hand, grip, and thumb opposition.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. A thumb on the plane of the other fingers, both surfaces of the palm flat, or joints crossing during grip.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
