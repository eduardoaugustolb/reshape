# Thigh

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 8.16, femur and patella](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-4-bones-of-the-lower-limb). Locate the proximal and distal changes in bony width.
- [OpenStax Figures 11.29 and 11.31, hip and thigh musculature](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-6-appendicular-muscles-of-the-pelvic-girdle-and-lower-limbs). Compare anterior, medial, and posterior muscle groups.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Arrange anterior, medial, and posterior masses around the femoral axis. Model the transition near the groin and taper toward the condyles.

## Evidence to capture

Inspect front, rear, and profile crops from pelvis to knee. In a flexed pose, compare the silhouette near hip and knee before evaluating muscle definition.

## Review and rejection

Check side and rear as well as front. Compare masses near the hip and above the knee in flexion if the target calls for it.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. A uniform cylinder or an abrupt groin or knee transition.
