# Ankle

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 8.18 and 8.19, lower leg and foot](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-4-bones-of-the-lower-limb). Compare the medial and lateral malleoli and the position of the talus.
- [OpenStax Figure 9.21, ankle joint](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/9-6-anatomy-of-selected-synovial-joints). Locate the talocrural joint and its flexion direction.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Use the medial and lateral malleoli as distinct tibial and fibular landmarks. Blend the joint into the dorsum of the foot and posterior tendon.

## Evidence to capture

Use front and both side crops with the foot attached. Compare neutral stance and flexion angles when a rig is part of the deliverable.

## Review and rejection

Inspect front and both sides for malleolar height and projection. Test dorsiflexion and plantar flexion if rigged.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Unjustified mirrored malleoli, a ring-shaped ankle, or a heel that detaches.
