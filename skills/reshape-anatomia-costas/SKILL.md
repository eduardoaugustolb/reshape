# Back and surface landmarks of the spine

Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Modeling

Build the back over the spinal curves and the position of the scapulae against the ribs. Relate trapezius and latissimus dorsi to arm position.

## Review and rejection

Check posterior and side views in the resting pose and with raised arms if rigged. Observe the change in scapular orientation.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Fixed scapular plates when arms lift, a uniformly deep spinal groove, or a lumbar region with no transition to the pelvis.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
