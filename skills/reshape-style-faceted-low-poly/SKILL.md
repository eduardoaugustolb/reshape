# Faceted low-poly modeling

Use [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md) for evidence-based approval and [the styles overview](../reshape-styles/SKILL.md) for mixed briefs.

## Visual references

- [Kenney Nature Kit visual sheet](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit?part=e79b84f8-9663-4137-a688-08360a0b0f3d). Inspect the rock and tree silhouettes and where large planar faces carry the design.
- [Low Poly Nature Kit preview by NS Studio](https://ronneda.itch.io/lowpoly-nature-kit). Compare flat-shaded foliage and rock planes at gameplay distance.

Use these examples to study visual decisions. Use the user's own target for likeness, dimensions, and design requirements.

## Modeling pass

Draft the contour before triangulation. Use face orientation to establish the main planes and spend triangles where planes turn, contours curve, or joints deform. Keep flat shading and designed sharp edges consistent with the target.

## Approval checks

Pair a wireframe with a flat-shaded clay render. Turn the model and check that adjacent triangles establish coherent plane groups. Measure the evaluated export against any budget.

Reject and correct these failures before approval. Random diagonal dents, fractured outlines, flickering facets, or reductions that remove defining landmarks. Record any remaining issue as partial delivery with a named view and cause.
