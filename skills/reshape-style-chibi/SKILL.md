# Chibi modeling

Follow the [required visual reference and review workflow](../reshape/SKILL.md#required-visual-reference). For mixed briefs, see [the styles overview](../reshape-styles/SKILL.md).

## Visual references

- [Good Smile Nendoroid comparison gallery](https://special.goodsmile.info/miku10th/en/). Compare how shape and accessory hierarchy change between scale-figure and chibi interpretations.
- [Good Smile Nendoroid Doll photo gallery](https://www.goodsmile.info/en/product/9401). Study the profile, balance, and separation between the large head and compact limbs.

Use these examples to study visual decisions. Use the user's own target for likeness, dimensions, and design requirements.

## Modeling pass

Set a subject-specific head-to-body relationship. Preserve the character's distinguishing features and reduce competing details. Resolve neck support, hand and foot masses, and clearance between the cheeks, shoulders, and arms.

## Approval checks

Check stance, profile, and rear silhouette. Inspect the face and hands at output size and verify that the head can turn through the intended range when rigged.

Reject and correct these failures before approval. A head that looks detached, fused limbs that interfere with expression, or recognition that depends on tiny costume marks. Record any remaining issue as partial delivery with a named view and cause.
