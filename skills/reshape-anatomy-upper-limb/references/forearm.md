# Forearm

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 8.6, ulna and radius](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-2-bones-of-the-upper-limb). Locate both bone paths and distinguish their ends.
- [OpenStax forearm muscle plates](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-5-muscles-of-the-pectoral-girdle-and-upper-limbs). Compare the superficial flexor and extensor sides.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Distribute proximal masses and taper toward the wrist. In pronation, account for the radius crossing over the ulna and for changes in surface contour.

## Evidence to capture

Keep the upper arm fixed in rig captures and compare palm-up and palm-down. Check that the distal landmarks move with the forearm rather than a twist concentrated at the wrist.

## Review and rejection

Inspect palm-up and palm-down orientations when applicable. Check distal landmarks against the orientation of the hand.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. A cylindrical twist that preserves the same outline, or rotation squeezed into a band at the wrist.
