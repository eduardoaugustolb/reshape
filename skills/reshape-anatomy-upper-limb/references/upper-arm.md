# Upper arm

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 8.5, humerus and elbow](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-2-bones-of-the-upper-limb). Use the humeral axis and distal widening to locate the mass.
- [OpenStax Figure 11.25, muscles that move the forearm](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-5-muscles-of-the-pectoral-girdle-and-upper-limbs). Compare the anterior and posterior contributions of the upper arm.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Model around the humeral axis. Distinguish the anterior flexor mass from the posterior extensor mass and transition them toward the elbow.

## Evidence to capture

Trace both upper-arm contours in profile with the elbow visible. For a rig, compare flexion to extension before smoothing or retopologizing the region.

## Review and rejection

Compare profiles in extension and, if rigged, flexion. Inspect the deltoid transition and spaces around the elbow.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. A uniform capsule or isolated muscle relief without continuity.
