# Thumb and opposition

For integration with the surrounding anatomy, see [the regional overview](hand.md).

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figures 8.7 and 8.8, hand and wrist](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-2-bones-of-the-upper-limb). Identify the first metacarpal and two thumb phalanges.
- [OpenStax Figure 9.10, types of synovial joints](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/9-4-synovial-joints). Identify the saddle joint at the base of the thumb.

## Modeling

Establish the first metacarpal base and thenar mass before shaping the thumb tip. Model the web space between thumb and index and match the orientation of pads and nail to the intended pose.

## Evidence and rejection

Check palmar, dorsal, and thumb-side views. If rigged, demonstrate opposition and a grip and inspect contact with the fingers.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. A thumb branching like a fifth long finger, an unstable base during opposition, or a web that tears in the required pose. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
