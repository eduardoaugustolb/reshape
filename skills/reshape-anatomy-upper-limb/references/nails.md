# Fingernails and toenails

For attachment and joint context, see [fingers](fingers.md) or [toes](../../reshape-anatomy-lower-limb/references/toes.md).

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 5.13, nails](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-2-accessory-structures-of-the-skin). Compare the surface and section for nail body, root region, folds, and free edge.

## Modeling

Build a plate curved to the distal digit and seat its sides in the nail folds. Give the exposed edge thickness only as the scale requires. Use the toe reference for toenail proportion and curvature.

## Evidence and rejection

Inspect dorsal and low side views with plain materials. Verify attachment during nearby joint movement if rigged.

Reject these defects when incompatible with the target. A floating plate, nails wrapping around the pad, or side margins that pierce the skin. Correct and recapture. Unverified mandatory evidence blocks approval.
