# Whole upper limb

Obtain the [required visual reference](../reshape/SKILL.md#required-visual-reference) before modeling or visual review. Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 8.3, pectoral girdle](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-1-the-pectoral-girdle). Follow the bony attachment from trunk to humerus.
- [OpenStax Figures 8.5–8.7, upper limb bones](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-2-bones-of-the-upper-limb). Trace the joint centers through elbow and wrist into the hand.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Define humeral and forearm axes and locate the joints before building volumes. Coordinate scapula, clavicle, and hand with the pose. Account for the movement of radius around ulna in pronation.

## Evidence to capture

Capture the complete chain in front and back at rest and pose. Verify hand orientation propagates through the forearm and that the shoulder remains integrated.

## Review and rejection

Inspect front, rear, and lateral views. If rigged, test shoulder abduction, elbow flexion, and forearm rotation during reach.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Twist concentrated in the wrist, an elbow without an axis, or a shoulder that separates during reach.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
