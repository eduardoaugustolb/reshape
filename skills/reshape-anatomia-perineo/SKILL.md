# Perineum and external genital anatomy

Follow the subject, pose, and style reference for form, variation, and asymmetry. Record landmarks and views for measurements. Use population averages as context and mark hidden or inferred regions.

## Visual references

- [OpenStax Figure 11.20, muscles of the perineum](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-4-axial-muscles-of-the-abdominal-wall-and-thorax). Locate the boundaries using the pubis, ischia, and coccyx.
- [OpenStax Figure 27.10, external anatomy](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-2-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-ovarian-reproductive-system). Use the labeled anatomical views for the structures required by the educational brief.
- [OpenStax Figures 27.2 and 27.3, reproductive anatomy](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-1-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-testicular-reproductive-system). Distinguish external contours from the illustrated sections.

Use the figures to locate structures. Compare external forms and pose against the subject reference.

## Modeling

Define the region against the pubis, ischia, and coccyx. Model the structures required by the documented target and distinguish the external surface from internal anatomy where a section exposes it.

## Evidence to capture

Use clearly oriented diagrams and one relevant view per required structure. State the variant and section plane, and mark structures outside the target as out of scope.

## Review and rejection

Check attachment, tissue continuity, and the relative position of relevant openings in appropriate anatomical views. Document variants using specific references.

Reject these defects when they conflict with the documented target. Structures inconsistent with pelvic attachment, merged openings, or unreferenced details labeled as verified anatomy.

Apply the saved-file review in [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md). Record the view and discrepancy, correct the cause, and recapture. Missing evidence for a required criterion blocks regional approval.
