# Clay and handcrafted modeling

Follow the [required visual reference and review workflow](../reshape/SKILL.md#required-visual-reference). For mixed briefs, see [the styles overview](../reshape-styles/SKILL.md).

## Visual references

- [Metropolitan Museum of Art Bust of a Man](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/206337). Inspect the terracotta surface, broad forms, and the scale of fine irregularities.
- [V&A Inside Aardman exhibition visuals](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/5-things-to-know-about-inside-aardman-wallace-amp-gromit-and-friends-at-young-vampa). Compare real puppet materials and where softness, seams, and components affect the read.

Use these examples to study visual decisions. Use the user's own target for likeness, dimensions, and design requirements.

## Modeling pass

Start with continuous hand-shaped masses. Match edge softness, compression, and asymmetry to the representative material. Place tool marks along plausible strokes and vary intensity so broad sculpted planes remain visible.

## Approval checks

Inspect a matte render in broad light and again in grazing light. Compare mark scale with the object and check assembly seams against the intended construction.

Reject and correct these failures before approval. Uniform procedural noise, disconnected pieces without a deliberate seam, or tool marks overpowering important transitions. Record any remaining issue as partial delivery with a named view and cause.
