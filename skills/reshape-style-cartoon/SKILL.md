# Cartoon modeling

Use [ReShape](../reshape/SKILL.md) for evidence-based approval and [the styles overview](../reshape-estilos/SKILL.md) for mixed briefs.

## Visual references

- [Blender Studio Sprite Fright simplicity study](https://studio.blender.org/blog/sprite-fright-the-illusion-of-simplicity/). Study how large shapes and gaps remain readable when tiny details are removed.
- [Blender Studio Wing It production](https://studio.blender.org/projects/wing-it/?asset=6420). Study silhouette, pose accents, and the relationship of gesture to camera.

Use these examples to study visual decisions. Use the user's own target for likeness, dimensions, and design requirements.

## Modeling pass

Design the line of action and negative spaces first. Use exaggerated masses and tapers to direct the eye and preserve a coherent joint under each transition. Build expression as a volume change and keep thin features readable at the intended scale.

## Approval checks

Test a plain silhouette at the intended size, then check profile and three-quarter poses. Compare expression, intersections, and volumes in motion if the asset is rigged.

Reject and correct these failures before approval. A pose readable only from the hero camera, arbitrary exaggeration unrelated to gesture, or forms that separate or collapse in motion. Record any remaining issue as partial delivery with a named view and cause.
